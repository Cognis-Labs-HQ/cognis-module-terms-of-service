# Legal Documents

Read published legal documents and give explicit consent to their current versions.

## What You Can Do

Open legal documents from footer links and review required agreements. Administrators can draft, preview and publish documents and inspect consent reports.

## Before You Start

Public legal text can be read without signing in. Consent belongs to the signed-in account; publication and account-wide reports require administrator access.

## Get Started

Open a legal link, read its current text and explicitly accept when Cognis requests consent. Administrators use the legal-documents section to add or edit a draft, preview it and choose when to publish.
