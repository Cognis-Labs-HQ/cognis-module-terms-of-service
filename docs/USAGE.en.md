# Usage

Read published legal documents and give explicit consent to their current versions.

## Prerequisites

Public legal text can be read without signing in. Consent belongs to the signed-in account; publication and account-wide reports require administrator access.

## Examples

Open a legal link, read its current text and explicitly accept when Cognis requests consent. Administrators use the legal-documents section to add or edit a draft, preview it and choose when to publish.

The play introduces the legal document and its navigation without accepting or changing consent. It requires an authenticated online session.

## Expected Results

Consent records the account’s acceptance of an exact published version. A new required version needs new acceptance; a tutorial or preview never accepts or publishes on the user’s behalf.

## Troubleshooting

Required acceptance needs an online server check. If offline access is denied, reconnect and review current requirements; cached text alone is not proof of acceptance.
